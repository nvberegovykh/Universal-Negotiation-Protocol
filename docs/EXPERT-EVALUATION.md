# Professional SI-assisted evaluation

## Meaning and limits

The author's term **SI** is retained for a proposed advanced synthetic/superintelligent advisory capability. This draft does not assert that a certified, generally reliable superintelligence is available. A deployment must name the actual system, its capabilities, validation results, limitations, and human accountability. It may use conventional analytical tools and AI where appropriate; if the approved mandate requires a capability that is unavailable, record that requirement as unmet.

Every proposal is entitled to a fair evaluation under the approved rules. No expert is required to endorse an unsupported proposal. Professional support consists of a reasoned, auditable assessment; eligibility for adoption requires the assessment criteria to be satisfied.

This repository itself has not received independent professional or SI certification.

## Review layers

1. **Formal review:** arithmetic, units, internal consistency, definitions, constraints, and whether conclusions follow from stated premises.
2. **Empirical review:** relevant current observations, source reliability, missing evidence, alternative explanations, and sensitivity to uncertainty.
3. **Domain review:** legal context, humanitarian consequences, security, economics, infrastructure, ecology, and cultural interpretation as relevant to the particular conflict.
4. **Implementation review:** capabilities, dependencies, incentives, milestones, verification, failure modes, and agreed remedies.
5. **Independent challenge:** evidence or reasoning that could invalidate the assessment, handled by reviewers with disclosed independence.

Each mandatory domain has an identified qualified professional and a scope of competence. SI/AI output is an analysis input. It cannot impersonate a licensed professional, supply a forged signature, invent field access, or confer legal authority.

## Current global data

“Current global data” means a documented search for relevant local, regional, and international evidence with explicit coverage and time limits. It cannot mean perfect access to all facts worldwide.

Each source record includes its origin, publisher, geographic scope, observation time, publication time, retrieval time, latest verified update, freshness limit, integrity reference, access limits, and which claims it supports. Source independence and conflicting reports must be assessed; a large number of copies of one claim is not independent corroboration.

Freshness budgets depend on the variable. Fast-changing access conditions may need more recent evidence than a stable technical specification. The guidelines set these budgets before the cycle. Evidence that expires before closure must be refreshed and the affected evaluation renewed, or adoption is ineligible. The hard deadline still applies.

A frozen snapshot makes the analysis reproducible. A parallel material-change watch checks whether the snapshot remains fit for the decision. New material information can invalidate eligibility; it does not create an unlimited correction loop. A later cycle can use the new snapshot.

Distinguish observation, inference, assumption, prediction, and value judgment. Where a fact cannot be verified, record an explicit uncertainty and its decision impact. Numerical confidence must have a documented interpretation and calibration basis; a model's unsupported percentage is not evidence.

## Required evaluation record

- Proposal identifier, full content digest, evaluation identifier and version.
- Guideline identifier, version, and approving agency mandate.
- Named responsible professionals, roles, competence, conflicts, and authentic signatures.
- Actual SI/AI model/tool identifiers, versions, configurations, retrieval scope, and material prompts or analytical steps, subject to the confidentiality policy.
- Evidence snapshot and source-level freshness/coverage records.
- Assumptions, calculations, alternatives, uncertainty, stakeholder impacts, and foreseeable failure modes.
- Disposition: `supported`, `conditional`, `unsupported`, `insufficient_data`, or `outside_mandate`.
- Conditions and verifiable satisfaction evidence; validity period and invalidation triggers.
- Dissenting expert opinions and independent challenge outcomes.

A majority score cannot erase a documented material contradiction. An evaluator may conclude that the problem is presently undecidable within the data, mandate, and time budget.

## Proposal version binding

An evaluation applies only to its identified version. Any material change to meaning, quantity, scope, responsibility, or timing requires review of the changed proposal. Editorial normalization that demonstrably preserves meaning may retain the analysis, with a reviewed equivalence record and a new digest binding the final text.

All sides ratify the same final neutral text. AI-generated paraphrases, different translations, and inconsistent annexes must not silently become different agreements.
